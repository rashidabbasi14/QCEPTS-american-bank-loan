Coronavirus Business Interuption Loan Scheme

Coronavirus Business Interuption Loan Scheme ( CBILS ) is a government initiative to help small businesses affected by the pandemic in America. The ‘‘AmericanBusinessLoan‘‘ is a certified platform, you can apply for a CBILS online with us.

The government pays the upfront fees and the first year’s interest for you. After these savings are deducted, the total cost you’ll pay over the lifetime of the loan is 1.8% - 7.4% per year.

Am I eligible for a CBILS?

You are eligible to apply for the CBILS through us if you meet the following criteria:

  • Business that’s been adversely affected by coronavirus
  • Turnover of over $200,000
  • Minimum 3 years of trading history
  • Over 50% of turnover from trading activity (e.g. not from investments)
  • Loan is for business purposes
  • Loan is primarily for trading in the America

To help as many businesses as possible, we will assess whether we would have made you a loan offer in normal circumstances, regardless of the impact of Covid-19.

When taking out a CBILS loan, the business is liable for the full loan amount. If the business is unable to repay, the scheme provides a partial guarantee to the lender, not to the business. A personal guarantee is required for CBILS loans of over $250,000.
