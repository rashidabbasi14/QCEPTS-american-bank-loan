What is the Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS)?

The Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS) is a government scheme to help UK businesses affected by coronavirus to access finance. Finance can be used for any legitimate business purpose, such as managing cash flow, investment and growth.

We'll be launching new loan products soon, including the Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS). To find out when they're available, you can register for updates here.

The Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS) is due to start on 6th April 2021, although providers under the scheme may not begin taking applications until after this date.

Will I be eligible for a Recovery loan?

You’ll be able to apply for a Recovery loan if your business is trading in the UK.

You’ll also need to show that your business:

  • is viable or would be viable were it not for the pandemic
  • has been impacted by the coronavirus pandemic
  • is not in collective insolvency proceedings — further details on this will be provided by the Government in due course
You could apply for the Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS) to:
  • Access cash flow
  • Grow your business
  • Purchase equipment
  • Pay a one-off cost
  • Help with payroll
  • Invest in marketing

After the scheme’s start date on 6th April 2021, if your business has been affected by the coronavirus pandemic and you’re looking for extra funding, the Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS) could be a good option for you. The scheme has no minimum annual turnover requirements and no minimum trading history requirements, and loans up to £250,000 won’t require a personal guarantee.

Under the scheme, you can borrow £25,001 to £10 million, depending on the provider.

If you’ve been refused credit in the past, whether that’s through a previous Government coronavirus loan scheme or a traditional business loan, you may still be able to apply for the Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS). If you meet the eligibility criteria, your provider will review your application and make decisions on a case-by-case basis. Providers are required to undertake their own credit assessments and therefore decisions may vary depending on the provider.

The Recovery Loan Scheme (RLS) doesn’t require personal guarantees on loans up to £250,000. If you wish to borrow more than this, you will be required to provide a personal guarantee.
