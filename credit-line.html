What is Credit Line?

If you’re planning to borrow money for your business but you’re not sure about the amount you need, Credit line is the best option you can go for. Credit lines have higher credit limit, it is like a cash on demand service for your business. It gives you accessiblity and flexiblility. You will pay interest only when you borrow money.

Suppose you get a line of credit for $50,000 and you use 25,000 from it. You will only be taxed for the 25,000 and the rest of the credit is still available for you to use. After you return the borrowed amount, you get the full 50,000 in your credit line back.

How can a credit line help you?

A credit line can help you in the follwing ways:

  • Accessiblity to cash, on demand
  • You pay interest only for the amount you use
  • Boost your cashflows
