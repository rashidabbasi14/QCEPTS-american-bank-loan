Rates and fees

As part of the Coronavirus Business Interruption Loan Scheme (CBILS), the Government will pay the upfront fee and first year of interest for you. After these savings are deducted, the total cost you’ll pay over the lifetime of the loan is 1.8%-7.4% per year.

There are no early settlement fees either and, if you pay off your loan within the first year, there will have been no costs.

Late payment: If for some reason you are unable to make your monthly repayment on time, we may charge an administration fee of up to 15% of the repayment amount.
